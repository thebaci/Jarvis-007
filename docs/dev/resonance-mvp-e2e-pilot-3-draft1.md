# Warehouse receiving guide

For new staff who have never received a delivery. Go from step 1 to step 6 in order, do not skip any step, and do not change the order.

## What to prepare before the truck arrives

- A pen to write on the delivery slip.
- The warehouse ledger (to enter the figures in step 6).
- The phone number of Tùng, the warehouse manager.

## Receiving steps

1. **Count the packages as soon as the truck arrives.**
   Count the total number of packages (boxes, sacks, parcels) the driver unloads and compare it with the number of packages written on the delivery slip.
   Until the count is finished, **do not let the driver leave**.

2. **Inspect the outside of each box.**
   Any box that is dented, wet or torn goes in a separate spot, not mixed with good goods.
   Immediately note on the delivery slip that the goods are damaged (say which box and what kind of damage).

3. **Check every line of goods on the delivery slip against the goods actually received.**
   For each line on the slip, check 2 things:
   - **Item code**: does the item received match the code written on the slip.
   - **Quantity**: count what you actually received and compare it with the number on the slip.

   Finish one line before moving to the next. Mark any line that does not match (wrong code, short, or over).

4. **Write any shortage or damage clearly on the delivery slip.**
   If any goods are short or damaged, write it right next to the signature spot: which item code, how many short, how many damaged.

5. **Sign for receipt.**
   Sign only **after** you have finished counting and checking in steps 1 to 4. Only after signing do you let the driver leave.

6. **Enter it in the warehouse ledger the same day.**
   Enter the **quantity actually received** (the number you counted, not the number printed on the slip) in the warehouse ledger, and finish it on the day the goods are received.

## Common mistakes

- **Letting the driver leave before the count is finished.** A shortage found afterwards can no longer be confirmed by anyone. Always finish counting first, then let the driver go.
- **Signing first and checking afterwards.** Once signed, it counts as received complete and correct. Sign only after counting and checking are done.
- **Counting only the total packages, not checking line by line.** The right number of boxes does not mean the right goods inside. You must check the item code and quantity of every line.
- **Leaving damaged boxes mixed with good goods.** Dented, wet or torn boxes must be kept apart and noted on the delivery slip.
- **Shortage or damage but signing plainly, writing nothing.** It must be written clearly next to the signature, otherwise you cannot claim it back.
- **Entering the ledger from the number on the slip.** You must enter the quantity actually received.
- **Leaving the ledger entry for the next day.** Enter it the same day; if left too long it is easy to forget or get wrong.
- **Guessing when unsure.** If anything is unclear, call Tùng, the warehouse manager, before signing.

## When you are not sure

If you meet a situation that is not in this guide, or you are not sure what to do: **call Tùng, the warehouse manager**. Ask before signing, do not sign and then ask.
